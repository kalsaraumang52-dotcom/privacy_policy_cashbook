Summary: Mixo Cashbook is an offline-first bookkeeping application. We do not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal or financial information with external servers. All data stays strictly on your physical device.

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy governs the manner in which MixoVerse ("we", "us", or "our") protects your information when you use the Mixo Cashbook mobile application (the "App") on Android.

Mixo Cashbook was built with a privacy-by-design philosophy. The App operates completely offline and does not require an account, internet access, login credentials, or phone number to function.

2. Data Collection and Transmission

We do not collect any personal data.

  • No Cloud Servers: We do not operate any backend servers or cloud databases for this App.
  • No Analytics or Tracking: The App contains no third-party tracking SDKs, no behavioral analytics (such as Google Analytics or Firebase Analytics), and no advertising networks.
  • No Account Required: You are never asked to register, sign in, or provide your name, phone number, email address, or banking credentials.

3. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

All records you enter into the App are saved strictly in your device's isolated local application storage (using an encrypted/sandboxed SQLite Room database):

  • Financial transactions (income, expense, and transfer records)
  • Transaction titles, amounts, timestamps, payment methods, and notes
  • Account names and opening balances
  • Custom categories and cashbook names
  • User preferences (e.g., date formats, haptic feedback toggle)

This information remains exclusively under your physical control on your device.

4. Device Permissions

Mixo Cashbook is designed to minimize permission requests. The App does not require or request sensitive permissions:

  • No Internet Permission: Core bookkeeping and reporting operate completely offline without internet connectivity.
  • No Location or Contacts: The App never requests access to your location, contacts, calendar, camera, or microphone.
  • File Export & Sharing: When you voluntarily export your financial records (such as CSV statements, PDF reports, or password-encrypted backups), the App uses the secure Android Storage Access Framework and standard Android Share Sheet via FileProvider. These files are saved only to destinations you explicitly select.

5. Encrypted Backups and Data Portability

Mixo Cashbook provides a user-initiated backup feature:

  • Backups are encrypted locally using AES-GCM (256-bit) and key derivation (PBKDF2) with a password provided by you.
  • We do not have access to your backup password and cannot decrypt your backup files.
  • You are solely responsible for keeping your backup files and passwords secure.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Because all data is stored on-device:

  • You can edit or delete individual transactions, accounts, and cashbooks at any time directly within the App.
  • Uninstalling the App or clearing its storage through Android Settings permanently and irrevocably deletes all locally stored data.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is a general-purpose offline bookkeeping utility and does not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction).

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
